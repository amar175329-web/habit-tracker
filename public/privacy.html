Privacy Policy for Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker is a private, local-first habit tracker, sleep logger, and daily reflection companion. We believe that your personal routines, mental reflections, and sleep rhythms belong solely to you.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. No registration or email is required.

2. Local-First On-Device Storage

All logs and reflections are stored locally inside your device using browser IndexedDB storage.

3. Optional Cloud Sync

If you configure your own remote database (such as Turso), data synchronizes directly from your device to your server over TLS 1.3 HTTPS without passing through our servers.

4. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

Habit Tracker contains zero third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact: privacy@habittracker.app
