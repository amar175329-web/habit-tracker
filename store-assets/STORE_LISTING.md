## 2. Full Description (Google Play Copy)
```markdown
DailyOS is a calm, local-first companion built to help you master your daily habits, honor your natural sleep rhythms, and capture honest reflections — completely private and offline.

✨ LOCAL-FIRST & 100% PRIVATE
Your routines, sleep logs, and innermost thoughts belong to you. DailyOS stores all data directly on your phone in local IndexedDB storage.
• No mandatory accounts or logins.
• No cloud lock-in.
• Zero advertising, zero third-party telemetry, and zero tracking SDKs.

🎯 SIGNATURE WEEKLY MOMENTUM
• Triple-Ring Momentum: Visual feedback across Habits, Sleep, and Mind.
• Flexible Weekly Targets: Set goals for 3, 5, or 7 days a week. Scheduled rest days protect your streak so you build lasting habits without burnout.
• 3-State Habit Cycle: Effortlessly cycle between Pending, Full, and Half-credit completions with a single tap.

🌙 24-HOUR SLEEP DIAL
• Intuitive circular 24-hour sleep dial to visualize your sleep and wake arcs.
• Monitor whether you hit your 7 to 9-hour target without intrusive wearable or audio permissions.
• Log bedtime, wake time, and perceived sleep quality in seconds.

🧘 MOOD & DAILY REFLECTION JOURNAL
• Five tactile mood orbs with customizable feeling tags to record your emotional weather.
• Daily reflection: write freely today. Past entries lock permanently at midnight to preserve authentic snapshots of your life.
• Fast search and tag filters to rediscover your journey over time.

☁️ OPTIONAL PERSONAL CLOUD & JSON BACKUPS
• Want to sync across devices? Connect your own private Turso database in Settings. Data travels directly from your phone to your server over TLS 1.3 HTTPS.
• Instant one-tap JSON backup export and import anytime.

Rediscover your daily rhythm with DailyOS.
```
