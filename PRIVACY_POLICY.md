# Privacy Policy for DailyOS

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026

DailyOS ("we", "our", or "the app") is a private, local-first habit tracker, sleep logger, and daily reflection companion. We believe that your personal routines, mental reflections, and sleep rhythms belong solely to you.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or name to use DailyOS.
- **No Tracking or Profiling:** We do not track your usage habits, build user profiles, or sell any information to third parties.
- **No Advertising IDs:** DailyOS does not access your Google Advertising ID (AAID) or hardware identifiers.

---

### 2. Local-First Storage (On-Device)
- All habits, habit completion histories, sleep duration logs, mood logs, and daily reflections are stored exclusively on your device using client-side **IndexedDB**.
- If you clear the app storage or uninstall DailyOS without creating an export backup, your locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

---

### 3. Optional Cloud Sync & Export
- **Personal Cloud Backup:** If you choose to configure a remote database (e.g. Turso) in Settings, your data is transmitted directly from your device to your personal database endpoint over an encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) connection. We do not host, broker, or inspect this data.
- **Data Export & Deletion:** You can export your entire database as a standard JSON file at any time from *Settings > Data & Backups*. You can also wipe all logs in one tap via *Settings > Reset All Logs*.

---

### 4. Device Permissions
- **INTERNET (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used solely to facilitate your optional cloud sync and local webview runtime rendering.
- DailyOS does not request access to your Location, Contacts, Camera, Microphone, Storage photos, or Exact Alarms.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & SDKs
DailyOS contains **zero third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs**. We do not bundle Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, or any ad networks.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
DailyOS does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because the application collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies with COPPA and GDPR-K.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected in this document with an updated "Effective Date".
